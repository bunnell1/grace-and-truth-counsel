Grace & Truth Counsel

Welcome, Counselor

This is a prototype support tool designed for biblical counselors. It offers self-assessment, focused modules on common challenges, a searchable database of counseling issues (including those corresponding to major DSM-5 categories), and Scripture resources.

Biblical counseling addresses people’s struggles through the lens of Scripture—sin, suffering, the heart, and the gospel of Christ—while remaining aware that some symptoms may involve medical factors best evaluated by a physician.

What’s Inside

  • Self-Assessment — Identify current growth areas with a short interactive quiz.
  • Growth Modules — Guidance on burnout, listening well, balancing sin and suffering, boundaries, and more.
  • Counseling Issues (Searchable) — Biblical principles and Scriptures for common struggles, including those often labeled with DSM-5 categories.
  • Scripture Library — Verses organized by counseling themes for quick reference.

This is a working prototype. Content is sample material based on common themes in biblical counseling literature. Always test everything against Scripture and seek wisdom from trusted mentors. DSM labels describe clusters of symptoms; they do not define the person or exhaustively explain the heart.
