About

Grace & Truth Counsel is a preparation aid for biblical counselors. It is meant to be used with Scripture open, alongside prayer, the local church, and pastoral oversight.

What this site is for

  • Applicable Scripture and biblical principles for common struggles.
  • Session example questions and counselee homework ideas.
  • Printable and fillable tools that stay on your device.

What this site is not

  • Not psychology theory or secular counseling techniques.
  • Not a substitute for the Bible, prayer, or the local church.
  • Entered notes and form data stay on your device and are not uploaded.

Add to Home Screen

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
